# How a person judges a plan (plan/2026-11-04b §8.4)

Read the two plans side by side without labels. Judge each on Murch's order (emotion first, rhythm and space last),
1 to 5 each, and then on the one question that matters.

| # | Lens | Ask | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emotion | Do I feel what the scene is about, at the moment it turns? | I watch a reading of the script | I am in it |
| 2 | Story | Does every cut tell me something I need next? Is anything invented? | Padding, or story that is not the script's | Every shot earns its place; nothing added to the story |
| 3 | Rhythm | Does the pace breathe with the feeling? Is there one deliberate break? | Monotone, or restless | The silence lands; the tempo follows the feeling |
| 4 | Eye and space | Do the eyelines meet, does the frame guide the eye, do I know where everyone is? | I am lost | I never think about it |
| 5 | Sound | Does what I hear carry the feeling the picture cannot? | Sound just plays | Sound narrows, drops or arrives early with purpose |
| 6 | **"I felt I was there"** | Overall | A reading | I was in the room |

**The mute test.** Mute the dialogue. Can you still read the emotional story? Score 1-5. The target is 4 or more.

**Which one would a Hiru director be proud of?** Choose A or B; "neither" is allowed. This is the figure the Phase 10
targets are measured on (Adam preferred over `script_split` on at least 80% of scenes).

Record each judgement in `ab_form.json` shape. The model critics' scores are notes for the person and never decide
alone (Chakrabarty et al., *Art or Artifice?*, CHI 2024).
