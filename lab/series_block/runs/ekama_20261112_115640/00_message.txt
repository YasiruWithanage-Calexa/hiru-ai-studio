DOCUMENT (numbered lines)
1: EKAMA SAKSHIYA
2: A 4.5-minute crime thriller
3: Sathya danne ekkenek witharai.
4: Colombo / Mahawewa
5: Action | Crime | Love
6: Evidence: C-17
7: Original screenplay and visual treatment
8: OVERVIEW
9: Genre: Action / Crime Thriller / Love
10: Setting: Colombo and Mahawewa village
11: Tone: Grounded South Indian realism with commercial action energy
12: Tagline: Sathya danne ekkenek witharai.
13: LOGLINE
14: A Colombo cash-transfer driver steals Rs. 300 million after witnessing his closest friend's
15: murder, then returns to his village with the woman he loves to expose the corrupt network that
16: destroyed them.
17: MAIN CHARACTERS
18: Dinu - 30, Colombo cash-transport driver. Quiet, experienced, violent only when cornered.
19: Aruni - 27, Mahawewa community nurse. Strong, intelligent and emotionally controlled.
20: Nimal - 34, Aruni's elder brother and Dinu's closest friend.
21: Mudalali - 52, businessman laundering illegal money through village development projects.
22: Suren - 38, Mudalali's brutal right-hand man.
23: Inspector Ruwan - 45, corrupt police officer protecting the money trail.
24: DIRECTOR / DOP VISUAL TREATMENT
25: Colombo palette - Cold blue, green and sodium orange. Handheld camera, tight frames and
26: crowded backgrounds.
27: Village palette - Warm amber and earthy red. Locked-off frames, natural light and longer
28: silences.
29: Love scenes - 50mm and 85mm lenses, shallow focus and minimal camera movement.
30: Action lenses - 24mm for geography, 35mm for movement and 50mm for emotional impact.
31: Lighting - Vehicle headlights, sodium lamps, practical bulbs and lightning flashes.
32: Editing - Fast cuts only on impact. Let important punches and reactions stay on screen.
33: Sound - Rain, breathing, metal impacts and footsteps first. Music enters only for hero
34: elevation moments.
35: Slow motion - Use only twice: Nimal's key falling and Dinu removing the gun.
36: ACTION DESIGN NOTES
37: The action is mass but not weightless. Every hit has a location, an object or a consequence.
38: Dinu is not an invincible superhero; he wins because he knows vehicles, space and timing.
39: Aruni is an active participant in the climax, and the final victory comes from the recording, the
40: village and their courage - not from violence alone.
41: SCREENPLAY
42: SCENE 01 - EXT. MAHAWEWA CANAL - DAWN - 1 MINUTE
43: Heavy rain. Aruni walks beside a paddy field carrying a small medical bag. She notices
44: something caught near the canal gate: a body. It is Nimal. She does not scream. She kneels
45: and touches his hand. Inside his fist is a small metal key marked C-17.
46: ARUNI - Dinu... oyata meka ahenne nadda?
47: She calls Dinu. No answer. A distant bus horn mixes with the rain. Cut to black. TITLE:
48: EKAMA SAKSHIYA.
49: SCENE 02 - INT. CASH VAN - COLOMBO - NIGHT - 1.5 MINUTES
50: Colombo streets shine under rain and sodium lights. Dinu drives a cash-transfer van. On the
51: dashboard is a faded photo of him and Aruni near a village bus stop. Aruni's voice message
52: plays.
53: ARUNI - Nimal thama gedara awilla ne. Oya ada enawa kiyala kiwwa. Mata dan
54: baya hithenawa.
55: Mudalali calls. Route eka maru karapan. Old Port warehouse ekata yanna. Dinu looks at the
56: red thread around his wrist and turns toward the industrial area.
57: SCENE 03 - FLASHBACK - INT. COLOMBO GARAGE - 2 MINUTES
58: Six hours earlier. Nimal opens a metal box containing photographs, bank documents and the
59: C-17 key.
60: NIMAL - Mudalali ge salli. Rupiyal million 300. Village project walin gaththa salli.
61: DINU - Api meka gaththoth apiuth eyala wage wenawa.
62: NIMAL - Aruniwa aran yanna oyata oni neda? Eya salli nisa nemei, oyage baya nisa
63: oyawa atharinnawa.
64: Nimal places the key in Dinu's hand. Dinu looks at it as rain hits the garage roof.
