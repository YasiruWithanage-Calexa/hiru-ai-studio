Every panel is a tall portrait 9:16 still of the same size, separated by thin, even, plain white gutters. All panels are the same moment's world, painted together: the same people, props, weather, light and colour grade in every panel.

People (every panel): every person is a smooth, matte, faceless artist's mannequin in one solid colour, with jointed limbs and simple hands: Anudi is the red mannequin; driver is the blue mannequin. World (every panel): One storyboard image arranged as four equal panels, two across and two down, read left to right, with plain gutters and no drawn text, numbers, symbols or arrows.
