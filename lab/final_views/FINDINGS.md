Faults (none is about the vehicle being wrong):
1. **The "Reverse" view did not hold.** Panels 2 and 3 named view 4 but the board painted the frontage (the sheet's
   most distinctive side) and the frames followed the board. The background is consistent, just not the named view:
   the board's per-panel view choice needs a check (the panel's background against its view) or a view the frontage
   side can stand for.
2. **The car straddles the kerb drain** in panel 2 and shot 2 (front wheel on the slab). The vehicles line's `where`
   should say what the vehicle stands on: "on the asphalt beside the kerb drain, all four wheels on the road".
3. **Dry shots came out damp** (shots 2 and 4): the wet sheet leaks despite "not its light, weather or wet ground".
   A sheet painted in even, dry light (the place_views lab asked for "soft, even daylight" but the roadside plate is
   wet) would carry less weather.
4. **Shot 1's driver** is large and sharp, not "soft at the edge" (as vehicle_role run 005); and with a camera looking
   back from the front passenger seat a right-hand-drive driver should sit at frame LEFT; he is at frame right (as the
   board panel). A side check for inside-vehicle shots is worth a line: "the driver's seat is on the right of the car,
   which is frame left when the camera looks back".
