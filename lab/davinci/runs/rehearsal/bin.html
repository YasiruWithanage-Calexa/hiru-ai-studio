The intended cut

A reference for the editor, not a binding edit.

A street-food vendor returns a stranger's phone and, for one rainy minute, the two of them forget their worlds.

Wide for the world, long lenses for intimacy; a still camera except where a thing falls or someone runs.

Key image: Anudi small in the rain, walking away, Kasun still behind his stall.

Geography: Kasun's stall is screen left; the road and her car are screen right; she crosses toward him and leaves to the right.
