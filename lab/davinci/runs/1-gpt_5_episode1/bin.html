A reference for the editor, not a binding edit.

In monsoon Colombo, a humble tea-seller returns a stranger’s fallen phone; a spark flickers until her father’s call ends it, leaving an ambition burning in the rain.

Build intimacy through progressive compression: begin wide, then move into OTS and singles. Keep the father disembodied. Movement stays along the curb; we never enter the road dangerously. Lines of sight: Kasun usually frame-left toward road; Anudi approaches from frame-right.
