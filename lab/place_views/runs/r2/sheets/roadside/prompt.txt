A location reference sheet for a Sri Lankan television drama: six photorealistic film stills of ONE real place, laid out in a grid 3 across and 2 down, read left to right, then top to bottom. Every still is a landscape 3:2 picture of the same size, separated by thin, even, plain white gutters. Nobody is in any view: no people, no figures.

Reference images, in order: image 1 is the location plate for Colombo roadside. Take from image 1 the place itself: its ground, walls, buildings and fixtures, their materials and colours, and where each stands.

The place, one connected layout seen from six positions: A narrow stretch of patched dark asphalt road runs down the left side of the place with a shallow open kerb drainage running beside it and a narrow concrete pavement alongside the frontages. A run of weathered low-rise commercial shutters and one modest two-storey concrete building with a balcony form the frontage on the right; the building has a small lit shop under a blue awning and a compact vending/three-wheeler bay beside it. Concrete boundary walls, regularly spaced utility poles and overhead cables line the pavement and visually frame the vending bay. Surfaces are rain-wet and muted (grey tarmac, dusty beige walls, leaf-green paint) with soft early-morning or subdued amber shop light.
