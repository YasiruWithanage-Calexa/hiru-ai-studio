Reference images, in order: image 1 is the location plate for Colombo roadside. Take from image 1 the place itself: its ground, walls, buildings and fixtures, their materials and colours, and where each stands.

The place, one connected layout seen from six positions: A narrow concrete pavement runs along the right side of a patched dark-asphalt road, separated from the carriageway by an open kerb drainage with removable concrete slabs and grates.
