EKAMA SAKSHIYA
A 4.5-minute crime thriller
Sathya danne ekkenek witharai.
Colombo / Mahawewa
Action | Crime | Love
Evidence: C-17
Original screenplay and visual treatment
OVERVIEW
Genre: Action / Crime Thriller / Love
Setting: Colombo and Mahawewa village
Tone: Grounded South Indian realism with commercial action energy
Tagline: Sathya danne ekkenek witharai.
LOGLINE
A Colombo cash-transfer driver steals Rs. 300 million after witnessing his closest friend's
murder, then returns to his village with the woman he loves to expose the corrupt network that
destroyed them.
MAIN CHARACTERS
Dinu - 30, Colombo cash-transport driver. Quiet, experienced, violent only when cornered.
Aruni - 27, Mahawewa community nurse. Strong, intelligent and emotionally controlled.
Nimal - 34, Aruni's elder brother and Dinu's closest friend.
Mudalali - 52, businessman laundering illegal money through village development projects.
Suren - 38, Mudalali's brutal right-hand man.
Inspector Ruwan - 45, corrupt police officer protecting the money trail.
DIRECTOR / DOP VISUAL TREATMENT
Colombo palette - Cold blue, green and sodium orange. Handheld camera, tight frames and
crowded backgrounds.
Village palette - Warm amber and earthy red. Locked-off frames, natural light and longer
silences.
Love scenes - 50mm and 85mm lenses, shallow focus and minimal camera movement.
Action lenses - 24mm for geography, 35mm for movement and 50mm for emotional impact.
Lighting - Vehicle headlights, sodium lamps, practical bulbs and lightning flashes.
Editing - Fast cuts only on impact. Let important punches and reactions stay on screen.
Sound - Rain, breathing, metal impacts and footsteps first. Music enters only for hero
elevation moments.
Slow motion - Use only twice: Nimal's key falling and Dinu removing the gun.
ACTION DESIGN NOTES
The action is mass but not weightless. Every hit has a location, an object or a consequence.
Dinu is not an invincible superhero; he wins because he knows vehicles, space and timing.
Aruni is an active participant in the climax, and the final victory comes from the recording, the
village and their courage - not from violence alone.
SCREENPLAY
SCENE 01 - EXT. MAHAWEWA CANAL - DAWN - 1 MINUTE
Heavy rain. Aruni walks beside a paddy field carrying a small medical bag. She notices
something caught near the canal gate: a body. It is Nimal. She does not scream. She kneels
and touches his hand. Inside his fist is a small metal key marked C-17.
ARUNI - Dinu... oyata meka ahenne nadda?
She calls Dinu. No answer. A distant bus horn mixes with the rain. Cut to black. TITLE:
EKAMA SAKSHIYA.
SCENE 02 - INT. CASH VAN - COLOMBO - NIGHT - 1.5 MINUTES
Colombo streets shine under rain and sodium lights. Dinu drives a cash-transfer van. On the
dashboard is a faded photo of him and Aruni near a village bus stop. Aruni's voice message
plays.
ARUNI - Nimal thama gedara awilla ne. Oya ada enawa kiyala kiwwa. Mata dan
baya hithenawa.
Mudalali calls. Route eka maru karapan. Old Port warehouse ekata yanna. Dinu looks at the
red thread around his wrist and turns toward the industrial area.
SCENE 03 - FLASHBACK - INT. COLOMBO GARAGE - 2 MINUTES
Six hours earlier. Nimal opens a metal box containing photographs, bank documents and the
C-17 key.
NIMAL - Mudalali ge salli. Rupiyal million 300. Village project walin gaththa salli.
DINU - Api meka gaththoth apiuth eyala wage wenawa.
NIMAL - Aruniwa aran yanna oyata oni neda? Eya salli nisa nemei, oyage baya nisa
oyawa atharinnawa.
Nimal places the key in Dinu's hand. Dinu looks at it as rain hits the garage roof.
