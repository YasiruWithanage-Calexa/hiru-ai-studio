EPISODE 1 — THE FIRST MEETING
Approx. Duration: 1 minute
It was raining heavily in Colombo.
Kasun stood beside his small three-wheeler, selling homemade short eats and tea to office workers.
His clothes were simple, but his eyes were full of ambition.
“One day… I'll have my own restaurant,” he told himself.
Across the road, Anudi stepped out of a comfortable family car, dressed elegantly and holding an
umbrella.
She accidentally dropped her phone near Kasun's three-wheeler.
Kasun picked it up and ran after her.
Kasun:
“Miss! Your phone!”Anudi turned around.
Anudi:
“Oh… thank you.”
She looked at his little business.
Anudi:
“You made all this yourself?”
Kasun smiled.
Kasun:
“Started with Rs. 5,000. I'm trying to make it bigger.”
Anudi smiled back.
Suddenly, her phone rang.
It was her father.
Father:
“Anudi, where are you? Come home. We need to talk about your marriage.”
Her smile disappeared.
She looked at Kasun one more time.
Anudi:
“Maybe… I'll see you again.”
She walked away.
Kasun watched her leave.
He had no idea that this meeting was about to change his life.
