# How a person scores a plan (plan/2026-11-06 §7)

Read `runs/<n>/plan.html` for the plan, or `side_by_side.html` for a blind read (plans under the letters X, Y, Z; do not open
`side_by_side_key.json` until you have scored every plan). Score each line 0 to 3:
**0** missing or wrong, **1** present but thin, **2** good, **3** what a top crew would do.

| # | Question | What to look at |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Geography.** Do I know where I am and where everyone stands, and does the camera keep to its side? | the first shots, the staging, cuts between two people |
| 2 | **Coverage per beat.** Does every beat of the story have the pictures it needs: no beat squeezed into one shot, no shot with nothing to do? | the arc against the shots |
| 3 | **Reactions.** Does the face that receives an important line get its shot? | lines and the shots around them |
| 4 | **Inserts.** Do the objects the story turns on get a close shot that does something? | phone, tea, umbrella |
| 5 | **Camera language.** Does every move and lens have a reason? Is there depth: foreground, practical light? | `lens`, movement, `why` |
| 6 | **Colour arc.** Does the grade follow the feeling while the physical light stays true? | the colour script |
| 7 | **Sequence endings.** Does each sequence end on an image worth holding? | the last shot of each sequence |
| 8 | **Would I shoot this?** Is it filmable in Veo, and is it the version I would be proud of? | the whole |

Total out of 24. Two plans are **equal** when they are within 2 points. Write the scores and one sentence of why in
`RESULTS.md`. The gate (plan §7): this plan is at least equal to ChatGPT's on Episode 1; a repeat run scores within 1
point of the first; the second scene scores at least as well as David today.
