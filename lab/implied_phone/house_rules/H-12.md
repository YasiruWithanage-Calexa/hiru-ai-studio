id: H-12
topics: [objects, sound]
kind: default
why: A voice from a device reads as invented unless its source is seen; a planner that will not show an implied object leaves the audience guessing.
version: 2
---
Implied things. A script often implies an object it does not name: the device a voice comes through, the thing an action needs. Such an object is part of the scene, as real as a scripted prop; showing it, and the one small action that starts it, is not invention. A sound or voice that comes from a device begins after its source is seen. Decide from the scene where the object is and who holds it, keep that the same in every clip, and show it at the moment it matters to the story, close enough for the audience to read what it shows.
