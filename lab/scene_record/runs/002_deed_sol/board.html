● Nimal ● Saman · ○ prop

Shot 1 · WS master · 5 s · beat 1 (2) · in: first

Nimal sits screen left and Saman screen right across the wooden table. Two glass tea cups stand between them. Saman talks cheerfully while Nimal listens. The window sits at frame left and the oil lamp glows beyond the table. Saman's right forearm completely masks the folded paper from this angle.

camera A: 35 mm, seated eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 3.5 m wide (FS) · Nimal left, Saman right, two tea cups centre · key light from frame right

Shot 2 · MCU single · 3 s · beat 1 (2) · in: cut

Saman continues the cheerful conversation, looking toward Nimal offscreen left. His expression remains easy. His right forearm settles down below the crop; the folded paper remains outside the frame. Nimal is outside the narrow horizontal field.

camera B: 75 mm, seated eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 0.67 m wide (MCU) · Saman centre · S looks left of frame at N · key light from frame left

Shot 3 · MCU single · 3 s · beat 2 (5) · in: cut

Nimal leans slightly forward. His attention drops from Saman's face to a point at Saman's upper chest, offscreen right. His expression closes and his gaze fixes. Saman and the table surface remain outside the frame.

camera C: 75 mm, seated eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 0.78 m wide (MCU) · Nimal centre · N looks right of frame at S · key light from frame right

Shot 4 · INSERT insert · 2 s · beat 2 (5) · in: cut

The folded land deed projects from its partly concealed position at Saman's upper chest. Only that small area of Saman is framed. Nimal's right hand enters, closes around the exposed fold, and begins drawing it toward himself. Only Nimal's hand is visible; both faces and the table are cropped out.

camera D: 100 mm, seated eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 0.39 m wide (CU) · Saman centre, land deed centre · key light from frame left

Shot 5 · MS two shot · 3 s · beat 3 (9) · in: match_on_action

Nimal, screen left, completes the pull with the folded deed in his right hand. He draws back from the forward lean and stands fully at his place. Saman remains seated screen right, his smile disappearing as he follows the paper. Nimal drives his right hand down toward the table center. Both tea cups remain untouched on the near half of the table.

camera E: 65 mm, standing eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 2.1 m wide (MLS) · Nimal left, Saman right, two tea cups centre, land deed left · key light from frame right

Shot 6 · INSERT insert · 1 s · beat 3 (9) · in: match_on_action

Nimal's right hand completes the downward stroke and slams the folded deed flat onto the wooden table. His fingers release it and his empty hand lifts clear. Only his hand and part of his forearm enter the frame. Both tea cups and Saman are outside the tight crop.

camera F: 100 mm, standing eye, high, static · plan says the frame is 0.28 m wide (CU) · Nimal centre, land deed centre · key light from behind the camera

Shot 7 · CU single · 3 s · beat 3 (9) · in: cut

Standing Nimal holds his gaze down and offscreen right toward Saman. His jaw is set and his lips remain pressed together. He does not soften or look away. The deed and cups are below frame; Saman is outside the horizontal crop.

camera G: 100 mm, standing eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 0.43 m wide (CU) · Nimal centre · N looks right of frame at S · key light from frame right

Shot 8 · CU single · 6 s · beat 4 (7) · in: cut

Saman looks up toward Nimal offscreen left. With his smile gone, he admits that he sold the field to pay a debt he had been too ashamed to mention. His eyes briefly lower before returning to Nimal, and his shoulders settle inward. Hold on his subdued face at the end. Nimal is outside the narrow frame.
