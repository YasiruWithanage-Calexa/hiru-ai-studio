## Spine

- Goal: Aruni's ordinary walk becomes a silent confrontation with Nimal lying still beside the sluice. She discovers the marked key in his fist, then cannot reach Dinu.
- POV: C02
- Subtext: Aruni contains her alarm. Touching a familiar hand leads to an unfamiliar clue, and the unanswered call leaves her alone with it.
- Key moment (beat 2, shots 5, 6): Aruni's touch brings the small metal key into view inside Nimal's curled right fist; its marking reads C-17.
