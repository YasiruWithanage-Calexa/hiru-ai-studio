Plan board: Ekama Sakshiya E01-S01, The canal discovery

Drawn by code from the scene record: each camera's view at the shot's first and last instant (position, height, lens), the figures on their marks with a tick for the way they face, props as white dots. Not a painting: it shows what the PLAN says, so sides, facing, size, continuity across cuts and missing people can be checked by eye.

Shot 1 · WS master · 7 s · beat 1 (3) · in: first

Heavy rain crosses the canal and glossy embankment. Aruni walks from the right toward the gate, carrying the small medical bag in her left hand. Nimal lies still beside the gate, small in the wide composition and not yet facially resolved. Aruni reaches the nearer stretch of path and stops as her attention shifts toward him.

Shot 3 · FS pov · 5 s · beat 2 (9) · in: cut

From beside Aruni's position, Nimal is revealed lying still at the waterline near the gate. His eyes are closed and his face is visible above the near bank. Rain strikes the concrete and muddy water. His right fist is closed; nothing inside it can yet be seen. Aruni stands behind the camera.

Shot 4 · FS two shot · 6 s · beat 2 (9) · in: cut

Aruni takes the remaining steps along the path and visibly arrives beside Nimal. Without crying out, she lowers herself fully onto her knees. She places the medical bag upright on the path beside her left knee and releases its handle. Her left hand settles on her thigh as her right hand reaches toward Nimal's closed right fist.
