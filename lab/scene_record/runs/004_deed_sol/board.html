● Nimal ● Saman · ○ prop

Shot 1 · WS master · 6 s · beat 1 (2) · in: first

Nimal sits at the west side of the wooden table, facing Saman at the east side. The west window is visible at frame left, and the oil lamp glows beyond the tea cups. Saman talks cheerfully around the subject of the land while Nimal listens. Saman's right forearm masks the folded deed at his chest throughout this view.

camera A: 24 mm, standing eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 4.65 m wide (WS) · Nimal left, Saman right, two tea cups centre · key light from behind the subject

Shot 2 · MCU single · 3 s · beat 2 (5) · in: cut

Nimal's face is seen from the front at a slight angle. His attention leaves Saman's face and settles lower; his jaw tightens. Saman remains outside the frame and lowers his concealing forearm during this held reaction. The tabletop and its props are below the crop.

camera B: 65 mm, seated eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 0.88 m wide (MCU) · Nimal centre · N looks right of frame at S · key light from frame right

Shot 3 · INSERT insert · 2 s · beat 2 (5) · in: cut

From Nimal's side of the table, a tight crop reveals the folded deed projecting from Saman's chest pocket. It remains folded, with no written face exposed. Only Saman's upper torso is visible. Nimal's right hand enters from the west, closes around the projecting fold and begins to pull; Nimal is visible only as hand and forearm.

camera C: 100 mm, seated eye, eye, static · plan says the frame is 0.37 m wide (CU) · Saman centre, land deed centre · key light from frame left

Shot 4 · MLS two shot · 4 s · beat 3 (9) · in: match_on_action

Repeat the closing grip as Nimal leans across the table and pulls the folded deed free with his right hand. He straightens and rises in place, keeping the paper in that hand. Saman stays seated, his smile fading as he follows Nimal. Once upright, Nimal drives the deed downward toward the near middle of the tabletop. The frame accommodates his standing face and Saman's seated face without a camera move.
