   7| Original screenplay and visual treatmentOVERVIEW
   8| Genre: Action / Crime Thriller / Love
   9| Setting: Colombo and Mahawewa village
  10| Tone: Grounded South Indian realism with commercial action energy
  11| Tagline: Sathya danne ekkenek witharai.
  12| LOGLINE
  13| A Colombo cash-transfer driver steals Rs. 300 million after witnessing his closest friend's
  14| murder, then returns to his village with the woman he loves to expose the corrupt network that
  15| destroyed them.
  16| MAIN CHARACTERS
  17| Dinu - 30, Colombo cash-transport driver. Quiet, experienced, violent only when cornered.
  18| Aruni - 27, Mahawewa community nurse. Strong, intelligent and emotionally controlled.
  19| Nimal - 34, Aruni's elder brother and Dinu's closest friend.
  20| Mudalali - 52, businessman laundering illegal money through village development projects.
  21| Suren - 38, Mudalali's brutal right-hand man.
  22| Inspector Ruwan - 45, corrupt police officer protecting the money trail.
  23| DIRECTOR / DOP VISUAL TREATMENT
  24| Colombo palette - Cold blue, green and sodium orange. Handheld camera, tight frames and
  25| crowded backgrounds.
  26| Village palette - Warm amber and earthy red. Locked-off frames, natural light and longer
  27| silences.
  28| Love scenes - 50mm and 85mm lenses, shallow focus and minimal camera movement.
  29| Action lenses - 24mm for geography, 35mm for movement and 50mm for emotional impact.
  30| Lighting - Vehicle headlights, sodium lamps, practical bulbs and lightning flashes.
  31| Editing - Fast cuts only on impact. Let important punches and reactions stay on screen.
  32| Sound - Rain, breathing, metal impacts and footsteps first. Music enters only for hero
  33| elevation moments.
  34| Slow motion - Use only twice: Nimal's key falling and Dinu removing the gun.
  35| ACTION DESIGN NOTESThe action is mass but not weightless. Every hit has a location, an object or a consequence.
  36| Dinu is not an invincible superhero; he wins because he knows vehicles, space and timing.
  37| Aruni is an active participant in the climax, and the final victory comes from the recording, the
  38| village and their courage - not from violence alone.SCREENPLAY
  39| SCENE 01 - EXT. MAHAWEWA CANAL - DAWN - 1 MINUTE
  40| Heavy rain. Aruni walks beside a paddy field carrying a small medical bag. She notices
  41| something caught near the canal gate: a body. It is Nimal. She does not scream. She kneels
  42| and touches his hand. Inside his fist is a small metal key marked C-17.
  43| ARUNI - Dinu... oyata meka ahenne nadda?
  44| She calls Dinu. No answer. A distant bus horn mixes with the rain. Cut to black. TITLE:
  45| EKAMA SAKSHIYA.
  46| SCENE 02 - INT. CASH VAN - COLOMBO - NIGHT - 1.5 MINUTES
  47| Colombo streets shine under rain and sodium lights. Dinu drives a cash-transfer van. On the
  48| dashboard is a faded photo of him and Aruni near a village bus stop. Aruni's voice message
  49| plays.
  50| ARUNI - Nimal thama gedara awilla ne. Oya ada enawa kiyala kiwwa. Mata dan
  51| baya hithenawa.
  52| Mudalali calls. Route eka maru karapan. Old Port warehouse ekata yanna. Dinu looks at the
  53| red thread around his wrist and turns toward the industrial area.
  54| SCENE 03 - FLASHBACK - INT. COLOMBO GARAGE - 2 MINUTES
  55| Six hours earlier. Nimal opens a metal box containing photographs, bank documents and the
  56| C-17 key.
  57| NIMAL - Mudalali ge salli. Rupiyal million 300. Village project walin gaththa salli.
  58| DINU - Api meka gaththoth apiuth eyala wage wenawa.
  59| NIMAL - Aruniwa aran yanna oyata oni neda? Eya salli nisa nemei, oyage baya nisa
  60| oyawa atharinnawa.
  61| Nimal places the key in Dinu's hand. Dinu looks at it as rain hits the garage roof.
