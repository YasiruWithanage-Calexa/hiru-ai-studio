The intended cut

A reference for the editor, not a binding edit.

In pounding monsoon rain, a shy street vendor and a warm stranger spark a brief connection over a dropped phone, his quiet vow and her sudden call hinting at a future that neither yet sees.

Keep it intimate and grounded: one vision, restrained camera, rain and steam as texture. Let faces carry the scene; use a small number of clean angles that the editor can cut freely. When voices are off-screen (thought, phone, narration), stay on faces or place. No coverage bloat: one must angle per beat, with a few strategic alternates where the turn lands.

Key image: Rain beads across frame as Kasun, framed by steam from tea, looks up; an umbrella edge drifts in, two worlds about to touch.

Geography: Kasun’s three-wheeler stall is curbside on a Colombo roadside. Opposite curb: a family car pulls in. The space between is a two-lane wet road with tuk-tuks and office workers passing. The chat happens beside the stall, close enough to hear over rain. Her exit is down the same sidewalk, away from the stall.
