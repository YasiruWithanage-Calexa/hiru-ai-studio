The intended cut

A reference for the editor, not a binding edit.

In monsoon rain, a humble street vendor returns a fallen phone to a young woman from comfort; a spark forms, but a parent’s call breaks it, leaving him watching the rain as fate whispers change.

Naturalistic handheld and restrained dolly, rain-forward soundscape, faces favoured over coverage. One vision: cool Colombo greys and blues, with warm skin tones and steam from tea as the emotional counterpoint. Keep moves minimal—one purposeful motion per shot. Perform the spoken lines in clean singles; let reactions breathe in the same frames rather than multiplying angles.

Key image: Kasun, damp under the awning, eyes following her into sheets of rain while steam coils from his tea flask.

Geography: Kasun’s three-wheeler is curbside under a makeshift awning; counter and tea flask face the pavement. A family car pulls up across the wet two-lane road. Pedestrian flow runs along the stall side; puddles collect by the gutter. Sightlines: from stall to car across the road; approach path brings Anudi past the stall edge where the phone falls.
