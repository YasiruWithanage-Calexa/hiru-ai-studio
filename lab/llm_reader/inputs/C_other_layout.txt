PART ONE: The First Meeting
Theme: A small encounter can change two lives.
1. COLOMBO ROADSIDE – FOOD THREE-WHEELER  [approx. 28 sec]
(Kasun'S Everyday Life)

The city is waking up. Buses, tuk-tuks, motorcycles and office workers fill the streets.
Kasun prepares his small three-wheeler food business. He arranges short eats, fills his tea
flask and cleans his small counter.
An office worker approaches and orders tea.
Kasun smiles and serves him.

and old equipment but doesn't lose hope.

PART THREE: The Choice
Theme: True success comes from believing in yourself, even when everything is against
you.
1. KASUN'S WORKSPACE AND COLOMBO – DIFFERENT LOCATIONS  [approx. 32 sec]
(The Struggle)
Kasun is determined to secure the Rs. 2 million needed to expand his business.
A sequence of connected moments shows his struggle.
He visits a bank and discusses a business loan.
He sits with a potential investor and explains his business plan.
He makes phone calls to friends asking for financial support.
He reviews his business documents and calculations late at night.
Each attempt ends in disappointment.
The bank cannot offer the required support.
The investor doesn't believe the small business can handle such a large order.
His friends are unable to help.
Finally, Kasun returns to his small workspace.
He sits alone at the desk, looking at the supermarket order and the financial calculations.
He realizes that his biggest opportunity may disappear because he cannot find the money.
