One photorealistic film still for a Sri Lankan television drama, a tall portrait 9:16 frame: the blocking plate of a scene, which shows where each person stands. A single picture, no panels, no gutters.

Reference images, in order: image 1 is the location sheet for Colombo roadside: six named views of the one place, numbered left to right, then top to bottom: view 1 Wide; view 2 Looking left; view 3 Looking right; view 4 Reverse; view 5 Detail 1; view 6 Detail 2; image 2 is the vehicle sheet for Food three-wheeler: six named views of the one vehicle. Take from image 1 the place itself: its buildings, its fixed things and every place that stands in it, their materials and where each one stands. This still is painted from view 1 (Wide)'s camera: paint that view's place, the same things on the same sides. Never copy the sheet's gutters or layout. Take from image 2: ONLY the vehicle itself, exactly as shown: its make and model, body shape and colour, wheels, lights and windows, and its inside: its dashboard, steering and seats. Ignore everything around the vehicle in that picture (its street, ground and light).

People: every person is a smooth, matte, faceless artist's mannequin in one solid colour, with jointed limbs and simple hands: Kasun is the red mannequin; Anudi is the blue mannequin. A small raised ridge on the front of the head, where a nose would be, shows which way the head faces. Each mannequin has the person's real height and build and sits, stands and holds things exactly as a real person would. No faces, no hair, no clothes, no skin: only the mannequin colour. The place, the light and the weather are a real, photorealistic film still.

Props: every prop (phone) and every small thing a person holds or carries is a plain, smooth, matte white clay stand-in of the real object: its real size and outline, and nothing else: no screen, buttons, logo, lettering, pattern or colour. A vehicle is never a clay stand-in: every vehicle is painted real, exactly as its vehicle plate shows it, with its real colours. Each stand-in is held, carried or set down exactly as the real thing would be. The place itself is real: its ground, walls, water, plants, what is built into it, and Colombo roadside, Food three-wheeler, painted as a real photograph, never as clay.

World: Early-morning Colombo in soft overcast daylight, with fine rain falling and wet asphalt and concrete. Road light comes from camera right, with gentle shelter shade and a slightly darker vending bay; use restrained roadside greens, grey concrete, earth colours and soft highlights without a grade change. All movable props are real-size, plain matte white clay stand-ins without markings. The older compact maroon food three-wheeler remains real, with its black vinyl roof, faded panels, dark-brown trim, worn silver fittings, hinged service hatch and scratched stainless-steel counter.

Vehicles: every vehicle is real, never clay, exactly as its vehicle sheet shows it: the Food three-wheeler is image 2, parked in the vending bay under the blue awning, its cab to the left and its open service hatch at its right end, all three wheels on the concrete of the bay.

Ground marks: Small movements (a turn of the head or body, a lean, a shift of weight, half a step) stay within half a metre of the mark; the feet never travel further. Kasun (the red mannequin) stands on the concrete pavement half a metre in from the kerb drain, one step to the right of the rear of the food three-wheeler, whose open service hatch and the blue awning above it are directly behind him and to his left. Anudi (the blue mannequin) stands on the same pavement, the same half metre in from the drain, about 1.2 metres to Kasun's right, in front of the green building, with the pale boundary wall beginning just to her right. The gap between them is the same in every panel, about an arm's length and a little more between their chests, never touching: a closer camera makes them larger, never closer together. What is behind each of them follows from these spots and the panel's camera.

The camera: view 1's anchor spot on the road, about two metres out from the drain, at standing eye height, square on to the pavement; a 28mm wide, both mannequins full length with their feet on the pavement, the whole three-wheeler, the awning, the green building and the start of the pale wall in frame. Kasun stands with his chest toward Anudi (screen right), both hands empty at his sides; Anudi stands with her chest toward Kasun (screen left), the clay umbrella open in her left hand, the clay handbag on her left shoulder, the clay phone in her right hand at her waist.

Draw NO text of any kind. The people, place and everyday details are Sri Lankan.
