CARD: PEOPLE TALKING ON SCREEN
- Each line: to whom, why now, how (feeling + intention + one physical act). The line before it is what it answers.
- Two speakers in one clip: the second answers the first. Say if they answer straight away, after a beat, or cut in.
- The one who is not speaking listens with a small live reaction, never frozen, never mouthing words.
- Eyes go to each other (or to the thing they talk about), never to the lens.
- A short line in a long take: the people keep living after it (a smile, a look away, a breath), they do not freeze.
