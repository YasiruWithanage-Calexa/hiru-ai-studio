CARD: A PHONE CALL
- The voice on the phone is never seen. Say how it sounds from the caller's side (urgent, flat, warm) in `how`.
- The person holding the phone listens: the face changes as the words land. That change is the scene.
- When the person on screen answers, they speak to the caller, into the phone, often quieter than face to face, and
  their face may say something different from their words.
- Ringing, screen light, the caller's name on the screen: only if the facts give them.
