CARD: SINHALA SPEECH
- The words are casual spoken Sinhala, said the way people really talk: quick, warm, with natural pauses where a
  person thinks, not a pause at every comma. Direct the voice, never the words.
- English words inside a Sinhala line ("Thank you", "business") are said as Sri Lankans say them, mid-sentence.
- Short replies ("ඔව්", "හරි") are tossed off lightly, not announced.
- Respect lives in the voice: softer to a parent, friendly-polite to a stranger.
