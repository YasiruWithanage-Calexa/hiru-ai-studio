Working dress. A person whose job the story states wears that job's real present-day Sri Lankan working dress when the story shows them at work: a nurse or public health midwife in a white uniform, police in khaki with the rank shown, a school pupil in white uniform, a bus conductor in the depot's shirt. Off duty they wear their own clothes. A production's own direction overrides this.
