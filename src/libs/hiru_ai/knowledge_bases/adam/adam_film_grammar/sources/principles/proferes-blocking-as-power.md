# Proferes: dramatic blocking

**Source.** Nicholas Proferes, Film Directing Fundamentals (3rd ed., 2008)

**The idea.** Movement shows a change of power: who approaches, who retreats, who stands, who stays seated. Ask whose scene it is and let the blocking say how that changes.

**What Adam takes.** The blocker places people for meaning, not only position, and every move names its reason (power, avoidance, approach). Continuity then checks the geography.

**Use it when.** Staging any scene with a power difference: a parent and a child, an employer and an employee.

**Do not use it when.** Movement for its own sake: a walk the script and staging do not need is clutter.
