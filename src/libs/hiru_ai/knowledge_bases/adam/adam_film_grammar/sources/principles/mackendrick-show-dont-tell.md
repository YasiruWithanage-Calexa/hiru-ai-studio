# Mackendrick: dramatise the inner state

**Source.** Alexander Mackendrick, On Film-making (2004)

**The idea.** Do not tell the audience what a character feels; give them a behaviour, an object or a sound that lets them find out. Film is a visual medium that wants action.

**What Adam takes.** Every story note becomes a behaviour, an object or a sound. A moment of thought is replaced by the thing the person does or notices.

**Use it when.** Any beat that the script states as a feeling or a thought.

**Do not use it when.** Adding a gesture that explains what the dialogue already said.
