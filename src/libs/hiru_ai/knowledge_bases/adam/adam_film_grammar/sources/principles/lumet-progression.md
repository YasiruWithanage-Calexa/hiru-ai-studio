# Lumet: a progression of lens and size across a work

**Source.** Sidney Lumet, Making Movies (1995)

**The idea.** Choose a visual progression for the story (for instance, wider and softer early, tighter and harder late) and keep to it so that a change means something.

**What Adam takes.** The treatment's size arc, checked by code against each card's size, with a tolerance of one step.

**Use it when.** Planning a scene whose feeling closes in or opens out.

**Do not use it when.** A progression the scene does not need: a scene that stays at one distance is allowed to.
