# Dmytryk: a cut must have a reason

**Source.** Edward Dmytryk, On Film Editing (1984)

**The idea.** Cut when there is a reason: a look, an action, a line, a sound. Cutting on the reaction is often more powerful than cutting on the line.

**What Adam takes.** Every cut carries a motive (action, look, line, sound). A line may play over the listener. The score counts a motive for each cut.

**Use it when.** Choosing where to cut and whose face to hold.

**Do not use it when.** Cutting to hide a weak shot: a cut with no reason is noticed.
