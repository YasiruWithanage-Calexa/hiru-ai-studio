# Mascelli: the five C's

**Source.** Joseph V. Mascelli, The Five C's of Cinematography (1965)

**The idea.** Camera angles, continuity, cutting, close-ups and composition: the five concerns of a shot. Each shot has an angle, a size and a place in the cut.

**What Adam takes.** The fields of a shot card: size, angle, height, lens feel, movement, focus, composition. A card says all of them.

**Use it when.** Filling in a card so that nothing is left to chance.

**Do not use it when.** Writing the fields without a reason: each choice serves the purpose of the card.
