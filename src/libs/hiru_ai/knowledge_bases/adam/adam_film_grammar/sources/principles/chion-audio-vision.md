# Chion: sound adds value to the image

**Source.** Michel Chion, Audio-Vision (1994)

**The idea.** Sound does not merely accompany the picture; it changes what the picture means. A voice off screen, the point of audition (what a character hears), silence and sound arriving early all shape how we see.

**What Adam takes.** The sound map: subjective sound at the turn, silences, pre-laps, sound before picture. The craft score looks for a change of sound state at the turn.

**Use it when.** Deciding what the viewer hears at the turn, and where sound can carry a feeling the picture cannot.

**Do not use it when.** Music as a substitute for a decision: a swell does not replace a choice.
