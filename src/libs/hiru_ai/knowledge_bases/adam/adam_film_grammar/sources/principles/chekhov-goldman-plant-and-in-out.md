# Chekhov and Goldman: plant and pay off, enter late, leave early

**Source.** Anton Chekhov (the gun on the wall); William Goldman, Adventures in the Screen Trade (1983)

**The idea.** Whatever is shown early should be used later, and what is used later should have been shown. Enter the scene as late as you can, leave as early as you can.

**What Adam takes.** Plant and payoff in the treatment, checked by code. In and out points are named: entry and exit.

**Use it when.** Planning an object that matters, and the first and last card of a scene.

**Do not use it when.** Planting something that is never used; or starting so late that the viewer is lost.
