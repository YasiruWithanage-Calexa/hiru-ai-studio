# Ozu and the studio's editing notes: pillow shots and one rhythm break

**Source.** Yasujiro Ozu's pillow shots; the studio's own editing.md

**The idea.** A quiet shot of a place gives a scene room to breathe. A rhythm needs a break to be felt: spend one deliberate break per scene, and make it count.

**What Adam takes.** Atmosphere moments (a pillow shot) are allowed and labelled; the treatment names one rhythm break and the score checks that there is exactly one, on the key moment.

**Use it when.** A scene that needs a pause, or one that has run on one tempo.

**Do not use it when.** More than one break in a scene, or a pillow shot in a scene that is already slow.
