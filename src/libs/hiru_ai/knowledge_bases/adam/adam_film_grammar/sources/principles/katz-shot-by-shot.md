# Katz: design coverage from staging and point of view

**Source.** Steven D. Katz, Film Directing Shot by Shot (1991)

**The idea.** Coverage is designed, not collected: decide the staging and whose point of view the scene belongs to, and the shots follow. Storyboard to think.

**What Adam takes.** A treatment before shots, a named POV owner, and staging before cards. The shot designer receives the treatment and the staging, not just the script.

**Use it when.** Any scene with more than one person and a place; before choosing sizes.

**Do not use it when.** Planning shots from the dialogue alone: it produces a ping-pong of speakers.
