# Arijon: coverage patterns and the 180-degree line

**Source.** Daniel Arijon, Grammar of the Film Language (1976)

**The idea.** A conversation is covered by a small set of patterns that keep screen direction and eyelines consistent, and the line between two people is not crossed without a reason.

**What Adam takes.** The continuity step: the 180 degree line, eyeline pairs, screen direction and who is where.

**Use it when.** Any scene with two or more people facing each other.

**Do not use it when.** Following the patterns when the scene wants to disorient on purpose (cross the line deliberately and name it).
