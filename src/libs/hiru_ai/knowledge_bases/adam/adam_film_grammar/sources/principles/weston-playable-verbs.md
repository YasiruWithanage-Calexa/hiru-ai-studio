# Weston: playable verbs and subtext as behaviour

**Source.** Judith Weston, Directing Actors (1996)

**The idea.** An actor plays an action (to charm, to hide, to test), not a feeling. Subtext is what the person does while saying something else. Direct with verbs, not adjectives.

**What Adam takes.** Wants are written as verbs (to enjoy, to obey, to hide). Performance on a card is visible micro-action (a thumb on a phone), never a feeling word.

**Use it when.** Writing a card's performance, or turning a story note into something an actor can play.

**Do not use it when.** Adjectives like sadly or angrily; they are results, not actions, and the video model cannot film them.
