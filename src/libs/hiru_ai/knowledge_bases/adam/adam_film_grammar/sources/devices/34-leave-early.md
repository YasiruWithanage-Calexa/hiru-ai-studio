# Leave early

**Ask it as.** Where should a scene end? How do I end before the explanation? How do I avoid a flat ending?

**What it does to the viewer.** Ends the scene as the meaning lands, before the characters talk it through.

**Use it when.** Most scenes; end on the face, the nod, the closed door.

**Do not use it when.** When the scene needs the aftermath (a decision announced) to be understood.

**Allowed kind.** treatment exit

**Veo feasibility.** good. The final take ends on a held image, not a fade.

**On a card.** Treatment: exit: she walks out of frame and the camera stays. Last card is the ending image.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The scene ends on Kasun letting out his breath; the father's nod is the last thing said.
