# Foreground frame

**Ask it as.** How do I show someone watched, shut out or overhearing? How do I put depth in a flat frame? What is an over-the-shoulder for?

**What it does to the viewer.** Puts a doorway, a shoulder or a window edge in front of the subject, so they are seen through something: watched, separated, on the outside.

**Use it when.** Eavesdropping, exclusion, a barrier between people. Shoulders for conversation, doorways for distance.

**Do not use it when.** When the clean face is the point, or when the foreground would hide the performance.

**Allowed kind.** composition (card composition.foreground)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Describe the foreground softly (out of focus shoulder on the left).

**On a card.** composition: {foreground: Dasun's shoulder, soft; frame_side: left}, focus shallow, arrangement ots.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** We see Anudi's face over Dasun's soft shoulder as she answers the call.
