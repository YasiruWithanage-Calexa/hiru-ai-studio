# Callback framing

**Ask it as.** How do I make the end of a scene echo its start? How do I reward a viewer who noticed? How do I close a loop?

**What it does to the viewer.** Returns to an earlier image at the end of a scene with something changed.

**Use it when.** A scene or an episode that began with a strong picture; the changed element is the story.

**Do not use it when.** When the first image was not strong, or the change is not visible.

**Allowed kind.** plant (elaboration) with mirror composition

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Match the words of both prompts; make the change large.

**On a card.** The payoff card repeats the plant card's size, angle and composition; blocking_in_shot says what changed.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The opening bench wide returns at the end, one person fewer.
