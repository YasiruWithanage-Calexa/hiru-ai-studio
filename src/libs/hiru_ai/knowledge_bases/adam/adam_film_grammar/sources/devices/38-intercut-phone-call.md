# Intercut phone call (only when both are in the script)

**Ask it as.** How do I film a phone call? Should I show both ends? How do I cut between callers?

**What it does to the viewer.** Cuts between the two ends of a call so each reaction is seen.

**Use it when.** Only when the script shows both people on screen. Otherwise the far end is a voice and stays a voice.

**Do not use it when.** Never to invent the person on the other end: showing a caller the script does not show is a new character.

**Allowed kind.** plain script moments (no invention)

**Veo feasibility.** good for two separate locations; keep each take to one person.

**On a card.** If both are in the script: script moments for each end, cut_in motive: line. If not: the voice plays over the listener (the line over the listener).

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The father is only a voice on the phone, so his line plays over Anudi's face.
