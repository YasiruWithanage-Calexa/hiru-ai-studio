# Cut on the look

**Ask it as.** Why do I cut here? How do I make a cut feel natural? What motivates a cut between two people?

**What it does to the viewer.** Cuts when a person looks at something, to what they look at: the cut is motivated by their attention.

**Use it when.** Most reaction and reveal cuts; the default motive when no line or sound drives the cut.

**Do not use it when.** When the look is not clear, or when the thing looked at is not the next shot.

**Allowed kind.** cut_in motive: look

**Veo feasibility.** good. It is a joining rule between cards, not a render risk.

**On a card.** cut_in: {link: cut, motive: look} on the card that shows what the look found. The score counts a motive for every cut.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi's eyes drop to the phone; we cut to the phone vibrating on the bench.
