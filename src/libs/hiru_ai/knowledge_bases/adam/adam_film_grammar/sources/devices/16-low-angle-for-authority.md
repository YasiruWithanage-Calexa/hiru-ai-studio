# Low angle for authority

**Ask it as.** How do I show a parent's authority? How do I show someone in charge? What angle makes a person imposing?

**What it does to the viewer.** Looks up at a person so they seem larger and more certain. In Hiru drama, the family elder's weight.

**Use it when.** The father's verdict, a boss's decision, a moment when someone takes control. Keep it to the moment, not the whole scene.

**Do not use it when.** When the authority is false or being undermined (use eye level), or for every shot of the same person.

**Allowed kind.** camera choice (card angle)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Say 'low angle, looking up at his face'.

**On a card.** angle: low, size MS or OTS onto the elder, the other person's shoulder in the foreground.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The father says this is not what my daughter is used to from a low angle across Kasun's shoulder.
