# Push-in on realisation

**Ask it as.** How do I show someone realising something? How do I make a close-up feel alive? When should the camera move toward a face?

**What it does to the viewer.** A slow move toward a face while it takes something in: the viewer is drawn into the moment the character is.

**Use it when.** A realisation, a decision, a growing dread. Slow, 4 to 8 seconds, on a face that is not moving much.

**Do not use it when.** When the moment needs distance, or on a shot that already has a rack focus plus a big performance. Do not push on a joke.

**Allowed kind.** camera choice (card movement)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Slow push-ins are among the most reliable moves.

**On a card.** movement: push_in, lens_feel long, size CU or MCU, take 6 or 8. Write 'slow push-in' in the beat's camera.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The camera creeps toward Anudi's face as her father speaks.
