# Subjective sound drop

**Ask it as.** How do I put the audience inside a character's head? How do I make a shock land? What does sound do at the turn?

**What it does to the viewer.** Narrows the soundscape to what the character hears (breath, a heartbeat, one voice) so the viewer feels the shock from inside.

**Use it when.** At the turn of a scene: bad news, a realisation, fear. The world's sound drains to breath, then returns when the moment passes.

**Do not use it when.** More than once in a scene, or when the picture already says it. Do not use music as a substitute.

**Allowed kind.** sound choice (sound map; no added picture)

**Veo feasibility.** good as a sound note in the prompt (ambience drains, only her breath); verify live that the model follows it.

**On a card.** sound_map.subjective: [{moment, kind: narrowing, description}]; on the card, sound.ambience: drains to her breath. The craft score looks for a change of state at the turn.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** On the word marriage the lake ambience drains away until only Anudi's breath and the thin voice in the phone remain.
