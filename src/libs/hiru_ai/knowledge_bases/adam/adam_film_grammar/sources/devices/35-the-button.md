# The button

**Ask it as.** How do I end a scene with a final image? What is a button? How do I leave the audience with a feeling?

**What it does to the viewer.** A final image after the scene's point: a small, quiet shot that closes the scene's feeling.

**Use it when.** Every scene that has a feeling to leave behind; 2 to 4 seconds, often the empty frame or an object.

**Do not use it when.** When the previous shot already ends the scene, or the image would restate the point.

**Allowed kind.** empty_frame / insert_detail / hold (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. A locked-off image with a simple subject.

**On a card.** Treatment: ending_image and ending_moment; the last card holds it. The score checks the last card.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The untouched envelope on the empty desk.
