# High angle to make small

**Ask it as.** How do I make a character look powerless? How do I show submission? What angle shows someone being overruled?

**What it does to the viewer.** Looks down on a person so they seem smaller, younger or trapped.

**Use it when.** A person obeying an order, a child scolded, a character cornered. A slight high angle, not a surveillance shot.

**Do not use it when.** For a character the scene wants us to admire at that moment, or constantly through the scene (it stops meaning anything).

**Allowed kind.** camera choice (card angle)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Say 'slightly high angle, eye line a little above her head'.

**On a card.** angle: high, size MCU, one person. Pair with eyeline: down.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi says I will come, Thaththe from a slightly high angle, her shoulders small.
