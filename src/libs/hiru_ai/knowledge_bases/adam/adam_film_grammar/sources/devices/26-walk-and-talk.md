# Walk-and-talk

**Ask it as.** How do I stage a conversation in motion? How do I keep a long exchange alive? How do I show tension while two people walk?

**What it does to the viewer.** Puts a conversation in motion so the space changes and the energy of the dialogue is carried by the camera.

**Use it when.** A long exchange, an argument that cannot be still, two people who are not facing each other. Keep it to two people.

**Do not use it when.** In Veo for long lines (each line must fit one take), or with more than two speakers.

**Allowed kind.** move (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Keep each take short, with one line; track at a steady pace and keep the faces in profile or three-quarters.

**On a card.** A move moment with the staging move (why: approach or avoidance); movement track; one line per card.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Kasun and Anudi walk along the road and the sequence is cut into four-second takes, one line each.
