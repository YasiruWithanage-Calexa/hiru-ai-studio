# Standing up and sitting down as power

**Ask it as.** How do I show who has power in a room? How do I show someone taking control? What does standing up do?

**What it does to the viewer.** Uses height in the room: whoever stands, or stays seated, holds the scene.

**Use it when.** A refusal (stands), a verdict (stays seated), an elder kept at a desk. Plan it in the blocking.

**Do not use it when.** When the furniture does not allow it, or when both characters would stand without cause.

**Allowed kind.** implied_action / staging move

**Veo feasibility.** good. Standing from a chair is a clean action; keep it to one person.

**On a card.** Staging move: why power. A beat: he pushes the envelope back and stands. Angle low for the one who stands.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Kasun stands as he returns the envelope; the father stays seated, and the room changes.
