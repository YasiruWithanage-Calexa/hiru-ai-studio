# Sound arriving before its picture

**Ask it as.** How do I build anticipation? How do I make a cut feel motivated by sound? How do I introduce a phone call or a knock?

**What it does to the viewer.** Lets us hear the cause (a buzz, a knock, a car) before we see it, so the reaction shot and the cut are motivated.

**Use it when.** Interruptions, arrivals, alarms. Put the sound at the tail of the previous card and cut to the source.

**Do not use it when.** When the source is unexpected and should surprise, or when two sounds would compete.

**Allowed kind.** sound choice (card sound.effects)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Name the effect in the previous card's sound; the next card shows its source.

**On a card.** Previous card: sound.effects: a buzz heard before the cut. Next card: cut_in motive: sound. The score counts motivated cuts.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** A faint buzz is heard over Dasun's close-up; we cut to the phone vibrating on the bench.
