# Weather or light echoing feeling (only as given)

**Ask it as.** How do I use light to show mood? How do I use weather without inventing an event? What does the sky do at the turn?

**What it does to the viewer.** Lets the place's light or weather, as the script and location give it, rhyme with the feeling.

**Use it when.** Where the script says late afternoon, rain, dusk: frame it so it carries the mood. A cloud over the lake as the news lands.

**Do not use it when.** When the weather would be a new event (a storm that is not in the script), or an obvious pathetic fallacy.

**Allowed kind.** atmosphere (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good for light and calm weather; risky for rain or fast changes.

**On a card.** An atmosphere moment (no person acts), seen: the light as the script gives it. Keep to what the place card says.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The late afternoon light on the water goes flat as the call goes on.
