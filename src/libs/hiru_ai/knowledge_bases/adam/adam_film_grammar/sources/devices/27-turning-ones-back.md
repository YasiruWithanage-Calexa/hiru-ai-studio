# Turning one's back

**Ask it as.** How do I show a character refusing or shutting down? How do I show a rift? What body language says no?

**What it does to the viewer.** A person turns away in the frame: the refusal is physical and the face is withheld.

**Use it when.** A rejected plea, an unspoken no, a character who can no longer look. Often at the end of an exchange.

**Do not use it when.** When the back would hide the key performance, or when the character has no reason to turn.

**Allowed kind.** implied_action (performance)

**Veo feasibility.** good. A slow turn from a standing or seated position; keep it to one person.

**On a card.** A beat: action: she turns away; performance: shoulders set. MS or OTS from behind the turning person.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi's father turns and walks to his car without answering.
