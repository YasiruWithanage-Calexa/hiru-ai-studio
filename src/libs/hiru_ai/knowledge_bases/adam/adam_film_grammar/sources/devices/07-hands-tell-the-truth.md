# Hands tell the truth

**Ask it as.** How do I show a character lying or nervous? What do the hands do when the face is controlled? How do I show restraint?

**What it does to the viewer.** Moves the performance to the hands when the face is held: a thumb on a phone, a grip on a cup, a fist opening.

**Use it when.** Characters who control their faces: elders, people hiding fear, someone who has just made a hard decision. A close insert, slow action.

**Do not use it when.** Fast or complicated hand work (typing, counting money, sewing): keep the hands slow and simple.

**Allowed kind.** hands (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** risky when fast; good when slow and simple (one hand, one object).

**On a card.** A hands moment (who: the person) as an INSERT card. Write one slow action in the beat: her thumb ends the call and she turns the phone face-down.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Kasun's hands stay in his lap while the envelope waits on the desk.
