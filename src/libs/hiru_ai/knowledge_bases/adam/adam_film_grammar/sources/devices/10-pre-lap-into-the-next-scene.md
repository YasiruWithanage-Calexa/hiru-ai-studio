# Pre-lap into the next scene

**Ask it as.** How do I link two scenes with sound? How do I move from one scene to the next smoothly? What is a J-cut?

**What it does to the viewer.** Lets the next scene's sound arrive before its picture, so the cut feels like a thought carrying forward.

**Use it when.** When the end of this scene and the start of the next share a feeling or a cause. Needs the final cut to lay sound across cuts (Phase 9 of the plan).

**Do not use it when.** When the two scenes are unrelated, or before the editor can overlap sound. Without it, plan the cut hard instead.

**Allowed kind.** transition (elaboration; sound across a cut)

**Veo feasibility.** post-only. It is laid across the cut in the final edit, not rendered inside a single clip.

**On a card.** sound_map.pre_laps: [{card, into}] and card.sound.pre_lap: the next scene's sound. Until Phase 9, note it for the editor; the clip itself stays silent at the tail.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Dasun is left on the bench; the clatter of the next scene's kitchen begins under his last shot.
