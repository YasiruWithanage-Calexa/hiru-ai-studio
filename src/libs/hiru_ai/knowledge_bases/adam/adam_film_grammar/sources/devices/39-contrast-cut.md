# Contrast cut (warm to cold)

**Ask it as.** How do I show a sudden change of mood? How do I cut from joy to loss? How do I make a hard cut meaningful?

**What it does to the viewer.** Cuts between two images of opposite feeling, colour or sound, so the change itself is the point.

**Use it when.** A scene that ends in warmth and the next opens cold; a break in the middle of a happy sequence.

**Do not use it when.** Too often; or when the two images are contrasting only in an arbitrary way.

**Allowed kind.** transition (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Describe both light conditions clearly in their prompts.

**On a card.** cut_in: {link: cut, motive: action}; sound pre-lap or hard stop; treatment transition_out: cut from warm light to cold light.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Cut from the bench in golden light to the father's office under fluorescent tubes.
