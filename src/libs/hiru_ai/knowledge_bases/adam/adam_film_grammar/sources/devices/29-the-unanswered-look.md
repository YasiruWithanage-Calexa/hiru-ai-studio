# The unanswered look

**Ask it as.** How do I show someone who cannot answer? How do I show a feeling that is not returned? What do I do when a character looks and nothing comes back?

**What it does to the viewer.** One person looks at another and gets nothing back: no word, no glance. The silence is the answer.

**Use it when.** Unrequited feeling, a plea refused, a child looking at a parent. 3 to 4 seconds on the person who looks.

**Do not use it when.** When the other person's reaction is the story, or when the look is held so long it turns comic.

**Allowed kind.** hold / reaction (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Describe the direction of the look and that the other does not move.

**On a card.** A hold or reaction moment on the one who looks; eyeline toward the other, who stays still in the next card.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Dasun looks at Anudi as she leaves; she does not look back.
