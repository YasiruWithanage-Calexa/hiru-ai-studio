# Pull-out to isolate

**Ask it as.** How do I show someone being alone? How do I show a character left behind? How do I end a scene on isolation?

**What it does to the viewer.** A slow move away that makes the character smaller in a larger space.

**Use it when.** The end of a loss or an abandonment; a character who has just been told no. Often paired with negative space.

**Do not use it when.** When the scene should end close and warm, or on a shot with a lot of action to follow.

**Allowed kind.** camera choice (card movement)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Slow pull-outs are reliable; keep the end frame composed.

**On a card.** movement: pull_out, size from MS to WS, composition.negative_space: the empty bench. Take 6 or 8.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** As the car drives away, the camera drifts back until Kasun is small at the edge of the road.
