# Empty frame after an exit

**Ask it as.** How do I show the effect of someone leaving? How do I end a scene on loss? What do I show after a character walks out?

**What it does to the viewer.** Holds the place after a person has gone, so the viewer feels the absence in the room.

**Use it when.** The last image of a departure, a refusal, a death. 2 to 4 seconds, with the world's sound continuing.

**Do not use it when.** When the next scene starts at once on the same action, or when a person is still in the frame (use a button).

**Allowed kind.** empty_frame (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. A locked-off shot of a place; avoid asking for a person to vanish in frame.

**On a card.** An empty_frame moment (kind empty_frame, who: the one left, if they stay in frame), ending_moment of the treatment. Take 4 to 8 s.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi walks out of frame; the camera stays on the bench and Dasun, and the lake goes on.
