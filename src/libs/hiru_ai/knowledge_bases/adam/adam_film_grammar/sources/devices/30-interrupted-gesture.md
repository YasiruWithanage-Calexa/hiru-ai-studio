# Interrupted gesture

**Ask it as.** How do I show a character changing their mind? How do I show hesitation or being stopped? How do I show an action not completed?

**What it does to the viewer.** Starts an action and stops it halfway: a hand towards a door, a reach for a cup that does not arrive.

**Use it when.** A moment of doubt, a decision undone, a feeling swallowed. One gesture, 2 to 4 seconds, in an insert.

**Do not use it when.** Fast gestures; or when the stopped gesture would confuse the story.

**Allowed kind.** hands (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Describe the stop clearly (the hand stops an inch from the handle and drops).

**On a card.** A hands moment: action: her hand reaches toward his, stops, and withdraws. INSERT, 4 s.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi's hand reaches toward Dasun's sleeve, stops, and returns to her bag.
