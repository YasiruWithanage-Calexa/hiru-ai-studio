# Rack focus to separate

**Ask it as.** How do I show two people drifting apart? How do I move attention within a single shot? How do I push one character out of the scene?

**What it does to the viewer.** Moves sharpness from one face to another inside a shot, so attention moves without a cut and one person goes soft.

**Use it when.** A turn inside a two-person frame: one person withdraws or is overwhelmed. Pair it with a push-in.

**Do not use it when.** In a wide frame with many people, or when the focus pull would be the loudest thing in the shot.

**Allowed kind.** camera choice (card focus; no added moment)

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Say the order plainly (starts sharp on Dasun, ends sharp on Anudi, Dasun goes soft) and keep it to one pull.

**On a card.** focus: {kind: rack, from: Dasun, to: Anudi}, shallow depth, foreground the second person softly. Movement push_in.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** As the news lands, focus slides from Dasun beside her to Anudi's face; Dasun goes soft behind her.
