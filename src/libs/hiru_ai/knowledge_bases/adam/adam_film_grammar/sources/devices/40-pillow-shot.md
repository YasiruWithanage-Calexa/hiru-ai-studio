# Pillow shot

**Ask it as.** How do I give a scene room to breathe? How do I show the place? What is an establishing shot for in a quiet drama?

**What it does to the viewer.** A quiet shot of the place (a road, a lake, an empty room), without a story beat, that gives the scene room to breathe.

**Use it when.** The edge of a scene or a long quiet stretch. One, 2 to 4 seconds. Ozu used it; the house style spends one rhythm break on it.

**Do not use it when.** In a tight scene, or when it would be a second rhythm break.

**Allowed kind.** atmosphere (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Places in clear light, no people needed.

**On a card.** An atmosphere moment (no person acts), serves the first or last block; EWS or WS, static; take 4.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The lake at late afternoon, ripples on the water, before we find the bench.
