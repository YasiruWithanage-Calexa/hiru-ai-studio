# The long take on the actor

**Ask it as.** When should I not cut? How do I give an actor room? How do I let a performance breathe?

**What it does to the viewer.** Holds one shot through a performance so the viewer watches the person think; the cut would break the spell.

**Use it when.** A decision, a confession, a breakdown; the performance carries 6 to 8 seconds.

**Do not use it when.** When the lines exceed one take, or when the face is not doing anything.

**Allowed kind.** cut_in continuous (card link)

**Veo feasibility.** good up to eight seconds; the line must fit inside the take.

**On a card.** One card with take 8, movement static or slow push_in, beats timed inside the take, link continuous for the next.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi's I will come, Thaththe, held in one eight-second take from the breath to her lowered eyes.
