# Held wide in an intimate moment

**Ask it as.** How do I show respect for a private moment? How do I give a character privacy? When should the camera not go close?

**What it does to the viewer.** Keeps distance in a moment that would normally get a close-up: the camera does not intrude.

**Use it when.** Grief, prayer, a confession the audience should not crowd. A locked-off wide that lets the person be.

**Do not use it when.** When the face is the story, or the scene needs the viewer inside the feeling.

**Allowed kind.** size choice (card size: WS or EWS)

**Veo feasibility.** good. A locked-off wide is easy; the figures read small, so keep the action simple.

**On a card.** size WS or EWS, movement static, take 8, focus deep. Purpose: distance as respect.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Anudi sits alone at the shrine; the camera stays across the courtyard.
