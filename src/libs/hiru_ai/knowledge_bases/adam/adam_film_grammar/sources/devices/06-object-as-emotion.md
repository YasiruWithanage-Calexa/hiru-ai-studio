# Object as emotion

**Ask it as.** How do I show a feeling without the actor saying it? How do I use a prop to show grief or neglect? What is a cup gone cold?

**What it does to the viewer.** Lets a thing in the room carry the feeling: a cup untouched, a chair pushed in, a light left on.

**Use it when.** Quiet beats after a loss, a wait, an argument. One object, one shot, 2 to 4 seconds. The object must be in the script or on the location card.

**Do not use it when.** When the object is not already part of the place, or when it explains what the scene already says. Do not stack objects.

**Allowed kind.** insert_detail (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. A single object in a clear light, shallow focus.

**On a card.** An insert_detail moment with objects: [the object]. INSERT size, 4 s take, angle high or eye, shallow focus. Write the state (cold, untouched, steam gone), not the meaning.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** After the father leaves, Kasun's tea sits on the counter with no steam rising from it.
