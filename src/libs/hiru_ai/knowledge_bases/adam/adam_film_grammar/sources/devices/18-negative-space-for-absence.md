# Negative space for absence

**Ask it as.** How do I show someone missing? How do I make a room feel empty? How do I frame loneliness?

**What it does to the viewer.** Leaves a large empty part of the frame beside or in front of a person: the space where someone should be.

**Use it when.** After a departure, a death, a refusal; when the scene is about who is not there.

**Do not use it when.** In a scene of connection, or when the empty half is distracting clutter.

**Allowed kind.** composition (card composition.negative_space)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Describe the empty side exactly (the left half of the bench is empty).

**On a card.** composition: {negative_space: the empty half of the bench, frame_side: right}; static or slow pull_out.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Dasun sits on the right of the frame; the left half of the bench is empty.
