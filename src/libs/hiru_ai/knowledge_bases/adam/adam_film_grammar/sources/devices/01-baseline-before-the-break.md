# Baseline before the break

**Ask it as.** How do I make the audience feel what is lost? How do I show a character happy before bad news? What is the state before the turn?

**What it does to the viewer.** Gives the viewer the full strength of the feeling that is about to be taken away, so the break costs something.

**Use it when.** A scene that turns from warmth to dread, or ease to pressure, and the script gets to the turn quickly. Give the baseline its own moment: a face, a laugh, a hand at rest.

**Do not use it when.** When the previous scene already established the mood, or when the scene opens in the middle of the trouble. Never invent a happy event: show the state the script already gives.

**Allowed kind.** baseline (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Faces and relaxed gestures render well; keep it to one person in a close frame.

**On a card.** A moment with tier elaboration, kind baseline, who: the person whose state it is, serves the first script block. A CU or MCU card of 4 s, performance as visible behaviour (laughing, tucking hair), seconds_need 0 when it plays under a line.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Before the phone call: Anudi laughing with Dasun, close on her face, hair tucked behind her ear, so the call that follows takes something from her.
