# The delayed reaction

**Ask it as.** How do I show a character slowly understanding? How do I make bad news land late? How do I show someone catching up?

**What it does to the viewer.** Lets a character take a second to understand before reacting; the viewer watches the meaning arrive.

**Use it when.** A shock, a joke that lands late, a realisation after a quiet beat. Hold on the face before the change.

**Do not use it when.** When the character already knows, or when the delay would be read as slowness.

**Allowed kind.** reaction (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. Describe the beat of stillness, then the change (eyes first).

**On a card.** A reaction moment on the one who learns; take 4; performance: a pause, then the smile fades.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Dasun's smile fades a second after Anudi puts the phone down.
