# Silence before the key line

**Ask it as.** How do I make a line land? How do I give a moment weight? When should the scene go quiet?

**What it does to the viewer.** Clears the sound and holds the face for a beat, so the line that follows arrives with all the attention the scene has.

**Use it when.** Before a confession, a refusal, a verdict. One per scene (this is usually the scene's rhythm break). 2 to 4 seconds.

**Do not use it when.** More than once in a scene, or on a line that is not the point of the scene. A silence everyone expects is a pause, not a break.

**Allowed kind.** hold (elaboration) or sound silence

**Veo feasibility.** good. A held face and a locked-off camera; say the sound is silence in the prompt.

**On a card.** A hold moment (elaboration, kind hold) in its own card with sound.silence: true and rhythm_break: true. Treatment rhythm_break {moment, kind: silence} on the key moment.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** After the father's news, an extreme close-up of Anudi's eyes, locked off, only her breath, then her eyes drop.
