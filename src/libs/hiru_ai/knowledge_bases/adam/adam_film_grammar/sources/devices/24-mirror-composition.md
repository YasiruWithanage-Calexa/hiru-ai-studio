# Mirror composition

**Ask it as.** How do I show a change in a character between two moments? How do I echo an earlier shot? How do I show a relationship reversing?

**What it does to the viewer.** Frames a later shot exactly like an earlier one with one thing changed, so the viewer reads the difference.

**Use it when.** A scene that opens and closes in the same place; a gesture repeated with new meaning.

**Do not use it when.** When the first shot is not memorable, or the change is too small to see.

**Allowed kind.** composition (same framing, new meaning)

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Repeat the same framing words in both prompts; the model will not match exactly, so make the difference large.

**On a card.** Same size, angle and composition as an earlier card; the changed element in blocking_in_shot. The payoff of a plant often uses it.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The wide of the bench at the start, the wide of the bench at the end with one person gone.
