# Enter late

**Ask it as.** Where should a scene begin? How do I start in the middle of something? How do I avoid slow openings?

**What it does to the viewer.** Starts the scene after the beginning of the moment, in motion, so the viewer catches up and the scene starts alive.

**Use it when.** Most scenes; the first card is mid-laugh, mid-sentence, mid-gesture.

**Do not use it when.** When the audience must see the setup (a new place, a first meeting) before the action.

**Allowed kind.** treatment entry

**Veo feasibility.** good. Describe the state at frame one (mid-laugh), not the arrival.

**On a card.** Treatment: entry: mid-laugh. Card 1 beat: window [0, 6], action: they are mid-laugh already.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** We join Anudi and Dasun mid-laugh on the bench.
