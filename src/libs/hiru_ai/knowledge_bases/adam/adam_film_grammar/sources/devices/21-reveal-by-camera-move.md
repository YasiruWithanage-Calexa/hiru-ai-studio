# Reveal by camera move

**Ask it as.** How do I reveal something without a cut? How do I show what a character has not noticed? How do I withhold then show?

**What it does to the viewer.** A move (pan, track, rise) that reveals something already in the place, so the discovery happens in front of us.

**Use it when.** A hidden person, an unexpected object, the size of a room. The thing revealed must be part of the script or place.

**Do not use it when.** When the reveal needs complicated choreography, or when two cuts would be clearer.

**Allowed kind.** camera choice (card movement: reveal)

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Keep the move simple, one direction, and name what it ends on.

**On a card.** movement: reveal, beats: [{window [0, 4], camera: slow pan right, then it settles on the empty chair}]. Take 6 or 8.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The camera drifts from Kasun at the counter to the row of cups he has set out.
