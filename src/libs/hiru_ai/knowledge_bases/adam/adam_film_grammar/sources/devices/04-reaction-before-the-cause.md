# The reaction before the cause

**Ask it as.** How do I build dread? How do I make the audience anxious before something happens? How do I show someone senses trouble?

**What it does to the viewer.** Shows a person responding before we know why, so the audience leans in to find the cause.

**Use it when.** An arrival, a noise, a message, a glance toward a door. Short: a held face for 2 to 4 seconds, then the cause.

**Do not use it when.** When the cause is the surprise itself, or when the reaction would explain too much. Do not invent the cause: it must be the script's next block.

**Allowed kind.** reaction (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** good. A held face and a turn of the head render reliably.

**On a card.** A reaction moment (tier elaboration, kind reaction, who: the person present), serves the block of the cause. A CU card, cut_in motive: sound or look.

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** Dasun glances at Anudi, then his eyes flick to the bench a half second before the phone buzzes.
