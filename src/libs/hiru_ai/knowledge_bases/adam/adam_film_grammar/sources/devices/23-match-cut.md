# Match cut

**Ask it as.** How do I connect two shots with a visual rhyme? How do I move through time with one image? What links the end of a scene to the next?

**What it does to the viewer.** Cuts between two shots that share a shape, movement or position, so one feels like a continuation of the other.

**Use it when.** Between scenes or across time; when two images can rhyme in their framing and position.

**Do not use it when.** When the match is forced, or when the two images mean opposite things by accident.

**Allowed kind.** transition (elaboration)

**Veo feasibility.** risky. Both shots must be framed to match; describe the shared shape in both prompts.

**On a card.** cut_in: {link: match, motive: look}; both cards share a composition (the cup, centre frame).

**Example (a Hiru-style scene).** The cup in Kasun's hand at the stall dissolves into the cup in his hand in the restaurant.
